# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes constructive participation from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific when discussing code or ideas.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent while still addressing technical or community problems clearly.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Keep issues, pull requests, and discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or degrading language, personal attacks, doxxing, sexual harassment, deliberate disruption, and spam are not acceptable.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock conversations, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and productive.

If a situation involves immediate abuse or platform-policy violations, use GitHub's built-in reporting and blocking tools. Do not publish sensitive personal details while reporting a problem.

By participating in this project, you agree to follow these community expectations.
